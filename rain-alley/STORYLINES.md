# Three storylines for 「雨の横丁」 (Rain Alley)

The subject is `../index.html`: a first-person, real-time WebGL walk through a rainy Shinjuku
alley — shops built and lit procedurally, rain, steam, a walker with an umbrella, reflections on
wet asphalt. It is interactive (drag to look, space to pause).

Everything below is shot from the real scene, captured frame-exactly from its `#t=<seconds>`
still mode at 1920×804 (2.39:1).

---

## A · One take, one walk

- **Device:** the place is the character; there is no other subject.
- **Form:** one continuous take — the camera never cuts, walking 35 s deeper into the alley while
  its own glance logic (built into the page) turns the head at the signs.
- **First two seconds:** a wide of the lane at rest, rain crossing the two paper lanterns; the walk
  starts on the first frame and never stops.
- **Beats:** lamps light the lane → the walk → three glances that name nothing (lantern, wet road,
  the distant figure) → the end card arrives without a cut, the walk still going.
- **Look:** 2.39:1, cool night, warm lantern; small Mincho type at the left edge; grain 9%.
- **Sound:** rain, one distant bell, a low drone that swells once.
- **Why not:** a single 40 s take is the honest demo, but the scene's variety never shows.

## B · Three lights, three acts

- **Device:** each act is one colour of the alley: cyan 純喫茶, red らーめん, amber 居酒屋.
- **Form:** hard cuts on the music between 8 setups, each a slow push; type names the light.
- **First two seconds:** the 麺 lantern strikes on and the first cut lands on the downbeat.
- **Beats:** cyan → red → amber → triptych of signs → the walker crosses all three → end card.
- **Look:** same grade, larger colour-matched Mincho titles, one accent per act.
- **Sound:** a three-chord progression, one chord per act, rain over it.
- **Why not:** it is a trailer for a feature list, and the triptych slides toward the three-card
  default.

## C · Night rain study ← chosen

- **Device:** the alley is a photographed subject, not a story; the film studies rain, light and
  reflection in it. The only words arrive at the end, when the picture turns out to be interactive.
- **Form:** match cuts on shapes and light — a lantern passes → the wet road mirrors it → steam
  against a shutter → pink and cyan neon → the road again → the longest view — with the last
  take walking on under the title.
- **First two seconds:** the lane at rest, two lanterns, rain, the road holding every reflection;
  the walk already moving.
- **Beats:** lane at rest → a lantern passes overhead → the wet road and the distant umbrella →
  steam → colour → shutters → the wide at 夜の澪 → the deep view → the title lands on the walk
  that is still going.
- **Look:** 2.39:1 inside black bars; crushed blacks, cool shadows `#0a1a20`, warm highlights
  `#ffd9a0`; Noto Serif JP 96 px, tracking 0.14 em, `#f0eee5`; Inter 15 px caps, 0.34 em; grain 9 %,
  vignette 22 %.
- **Sound:** rain as the whole score — a stereo bed with slow gusts; one hiss when the steam
  passes; a 3 dB duck under the title. No music (no library available offline).

---

**Chosen: C** (the person picked it). A and B were offered in the same words.
